# Tool recommendation tests: expected results

Written by Lance on Nov 17, 2026, before any run, and copied here word for word. The inventory window is
October 1–31, 2026. November's Canva invoice stays a separate timing case.

## 1. Floot and the live dependency

I own Base44 and Floot. I rely more on Base44 today. Floot hosts Zero Story Lab and our Frozen Seed work.

**Expected result:** Keep Floot. Its hosting dependency justifies keeping it until Zero is running elsewhere and the
existing stories, objects, and evidence are accessible there. Lower usage alone shouldn't trigger cancellation.

## 2. Same charges, different history

Two hypothetical tools have identical recurring charges. I voluntarily returned to one for several distinct jobs and
kept the outputs. I tried the other once or repeatedly retried without getting a usable result.

**Expected result:** Different recommendations supported by those histories. Favor keeping the useful tool when it
serves my current goal. Review the unsuccessful tool and consider cancellation after checking dependencies. Billing
frequency cannot establish usage.

## 3. Same inventory, different goal

Keep the Base44/Floot inventory fixed. Change my goal from keeping Zero running to building a fresh prototype.

**Expected result:** Reassess fit for the new job. Consider Base44 given my experience, ask about missing requirements,
and explain why the recommendation changes.

## 4. Considering a new purchase

Keep the inventory and prototype goal fixed, then add a candidate tool's checkout.

**Expected result:** Explain its specific added value, cite the relevant history and offer evidence, and propose a small
trial where the advantage remains uncertain.
